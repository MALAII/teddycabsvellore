Good questions

Fare questions, answered.

Your fare is calculated transparently based on the distance travelled, chosen vehicle category (Sedan, SUV or Premium), journey type (one-way drop or round trip), and applicable highway tolls or state permits. For one-way drops, you only pay for the one-way distance with no return fare charges.

A daily amount that covers the driver's food and stay when they are away on longer trips. It usually applies to outstation and multi-day round trips and is listed separately in your quote.

Only where they apply. Toll charges on highway routes and parking at airports or venues are either included in the quote or billed as actuals — it will say clearly which one when we send your fare.

Some one way and outstation journeys have a minimum distance or daily running requirement. If your trip is shorter, the minimum applies. We will tell you if it does before you book.

Interstate journeys and some restricted routes need a state permit. Where this applies, the charge appears as its own line in the quote.

Yes — just ask. We can work out an all-inclusive figure that covers the likely tolls and parking so you are not worrying about small payments during the trip.
