Our story

We're from Vellore. This is our town, and these are our roads.

Teddy Cabs started with a simple idea: booking a cab should feel as easy as calling a friend who happens to own a car. Friendly, clear and done in a few minutes.

Who we are

A small Vellore team with a big soft spot for good service.

We are a local cab booking service based in Vellore, Tamil Nadu. That means we are not guessing what the roads are like or how long a trip takes — we have driven them.

Every enquiry that comes in is treated like a plan someone actually has to make. You need to catch a flight, get to a hospital appointment, take the family to a wedding or drop everything and travel to Chennai at short notice. Our job is to make that part feel handled.

We keep our promises small and specific: a clear fare, a comfortable car and someone on the other end who replies. That is the whole idea behind Teddy Cabs.

Our story

How Teddy came to be.

Phase 01

The Problem

Booking a cab used to mean endless phone tag: shifting quotes, surprise parking charges, and confusion by the time you hung up.

We kept hearing this from friends and travellers in Vellore — and we knew there had to be a better way.

Phase 02

The Solution

What if booking a cab was just a short, transparent conversation? You share your route, we give you a locked, all-inclusive quote with clear written details.

No sales talk, no hidden surcharges. Just straight, honest answers upfront.

Phase 03

Where We Are

Today, Teddy Cabs coordinates local rides, one way drops, outstation journeys and airport transfers from Vellore across 38 Tamil Nadu districts.

We stay proudly personal — because knowing our routes and travellers is what makes the ride special.

How we plan a ride

Five details, one clear plan.

Every enquiry we handle comes down to the same set of questions. Once we have the answers, the rest is straightforward.

  • Where we are picking you up — exact point, not just the town
  • Where you are going and whether you are coming back
  • The date and time you need to leave, with any deadline behind it
  • How many people and how much luggage is travelling
  • Which vehicle category you would prefer, and what is available

A note from the team

"We would rather spend two extra minutes on the booking than have you spend twenty on the phone at the end of the trip. If something is not clear, ask us. That is what we are here for."
