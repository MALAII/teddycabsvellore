Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

We like to keep things simple, including this page. Here is what happens to the information you enter on this website.

What we collect

Our booking form and contact form ask for the details we need to plan a ride — typically your name, mobile number, pickup and drop points, journey date, journey type, passenger count and preferred vehicle.

Right now these forms run entirely in your browser. Until a WhatsApp number or a server-side handler is configured, nothing you type is transmitted to us. The form prepares a summary you can copy and share once our contact channels go live.

How we will use it

Once a booking channel is connected, we will use your details only to check availability, quote a fare, confirm your ride and stay in touch about the journey you asked about.

WhatsApp

If you send an enquiry through WhatsApp, that conversation runs on WhatsApp's platform and is governed by its own privacy terms.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated before tracking is switched on.

Third-party resources

Fonts and photographs are served from this website rather than external services, so visiting a page does not share your details with a third-party content network.

Keeping your details

Ride enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep records of completed journeys. You can ask us to remove your details at any time using the contact page.

Changes

If how we handle information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
